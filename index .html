Free legal aid near you

Justice Centres Uganda

A free service for poor and vulnerable people, run under JLOS. National office: Chambers F6 and F8, High Court Building, Kampala. Toll free: 0800 100 210. Centres in Mmengo (Kampala), Hoima, Lira and Tororo, with service points in Jinja and Masaka. Tororo: Chief Magistrate’s Court, Uhuru Road, 0759 500443.

In every district you can also go to

  • The District Labour Officer (work problems)
  • The Probation and Social Welfare Officer (children, family)
  • The police Child and Family Protection Unit (violence, child abuse)
  • The Magistrate’s court and your LC1 or sub-county office
  • The Uganda Law Society, which runs legal offices around the country. Ask for the nearest one.

Coverage and phone numbers can change. Call before you travel.
